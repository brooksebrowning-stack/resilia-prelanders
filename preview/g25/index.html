WHAT’S REALLY GOING ON

The number is pressure. Underneath it are your arteries.

A blood-pressure reading measures how hard blood presses on your artery walls. What sets that pressure is the path the blood travels through.

With age, artery walls naturally stiffen and the path narrows, so your heart has to push harder to get blood where it needs to go. The number is the warning light. The arteries are the system underneath it.
