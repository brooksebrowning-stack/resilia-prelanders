STORY 1 · ANDRE, 50

The man who carried everybody

Twenty-two years married. On their anniversary, Robin left with a man half his age, and everybody had known for a year. Andre had spent two decades on double shifts holding everyone up, and he hadn't done a thing for himself in years. Then Solomon, 58, sat down next to him in a gym locker room.

"It wasn't your age, Andre. It was 20 years of carrying everybody."

Solomon trained with him most nights and handed him a pouch of aged garlic. Five weeks later, when Robin came for her things, Andre was heading out to the gym with Solomon. "Leave your key on the counter when you go."

STORY 2 · DAN, 43

The man who thought it was too late

Married, two kids, long weeks. Dan hadn't done anything for his own health in years and was sure it was too late to start. So, red in the face, he finally talked to his neighbor Joe, 62, who walks every evening.

"Almost every man I walk with thought he'd left it too late."

His plan: walk after dinner, eat real food, cut back the wine, and two softgels of aged garlic every morning. Six months later, he and his wife were walking together after dinner.

STORY 3 · MARCUS, 61

The man who tried twelve plans

Marcus had tried twelve plans in nine years: keto, fasting, the shakes, a gym membership he used twice. None of them stuck, because none of them were simple enough to keep.

"Pick one thing you'll do every day."

What finally stuck: evening walks, real food, and two softgels of aged garlic every morning.

STORY 4 · ANDREW, 37

The man whose family let it happen

Long weeks at two jobs. At Christmas he learned his wife and his best friend of 15 years had been together for three years, and his whole family knew. His brother Kevin got on the next flight from Los Angeles.

"Brother, we have work to do."

Sleep, real food, a walk every day with Kevin, and a pouch of aged garlic on the counter. On New Year's Eve, Andrew walked into the same family dinner with his brother next to him.
