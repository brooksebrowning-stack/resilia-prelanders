Where he'd let things slide

"I knew she was right."

So he started with what he knew: a walk every morning and the stairs instead of the lift. But he wanted one simple thing he'd actually stick with every day.

"Start with your heart, Mike. Every morning."

Raphael's view was simple: pick one thing you'll do every single day and make it about your heart. Walk in the morning, eat a real breakfast, and take the same two softgels he's taken for years.
