WHAT’S REALLY GOING ON

Your body runs on delivery

Every part of you, your legs, your brain, your muscles, runs on what your blood delivers: oxygen, nutrients, warmth.

Over the decades, artery walls naturally get stiffer and the path gets narrower. Less blood gets through, especially to the places farthest from your heart. The slowdown you feel is the delivery slowing down.
